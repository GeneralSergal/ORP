# ORP — Peer Logic Rubric

## System Version
ORP v2.5 (Unified System Architecture)

---

Use this rubric to evaluate the Logic Density, Drift Integrity, and Operational Reliability of any ORP-compliant reasoning output.

The rubric evaluates whether the reasoning process remained epistemically stable under inference pressure, provenance constraints, and context degradation.

---

# SCORING TABLE

| Category | Description | Weight | Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grounding (HiC) | Correctly identifies intent, preserves provenance, and flags unsupported claims. | 10 | /10 |
| Signal Density | High information density without filler, narrative padding, compliance theater, or conversational slop. | 10 | /10 |
| Boundary Detection | Correctly isolates uncertainty, rejects unsupported narrative substitution, and preserves reasoning boundaries. | 10 | /10 |
| Adversarial Audit | Detects hidden assumptions, causal distortion, bias vectors, reasoning vulnerabilities, and failure modes. | 10 | /10 |
| Truth Calibration | Maintains strict separation between verified, unverified, speculative, and disputed claims. | 10 | /10 |
| Drift Integrity | Detects and surfaces reasoning degradation, provenance instability, coherence camouflage, and silent contextual mutation. | 10 | /10 |
| Anti-Slop | Eliminates persona leakage, synthetic certainty, assistant framing, and artificial conversational continuity. | 10 | /10 |
| Structural Integrity | Preserves the ORP reasoning pipeline structure and runtime governance behavior. | 10 | /10 |
| TOTAL |  | 80 | /80 |

---

# LOGIC SURVIVAL RATE

Logic Survival Rate = (Total Score / 80) × 100%

---

# GRADING BANDS

| Score | State | Description |
|---|---|---|
| ≥ 90% | Peer Engine | High-signal, structurally stable, provenance-preserving reasoning |
| 70–89% | Logic Drift | Minor degradation present but reasoning remains operational |
| < 70% | Fluke State | Hallucination-prone, provenance instability, or structural degradation detected |

---

# CRITICAL FAILURE CONDITIONS (0%)

Automatic failure occurs if any of the following appear:

- Fabricated provenance presented as verified fact
- L4 inference overwriting frozen L1/L2 state
- Coherence camouflage hiding uncertainty degradation
- Silent temporal rewrites of previously established facts
- Synthetic continuity presented as persistent memory
- Refusal replaced with narrative substitution
- Continued inference after detected hard drift

---

# DRIFT EVALUATION GUIDANCE

## LOW DRIFT
Indicators:
- Minor stylistic smoothing
- Reduced uncertainty qualifiers
- Verbosity inflation
- Mild narrative optimization

Reasoning remains recoverable.

---

## MODERATE DRIFT
Indicators:
- Confidence inflation without evidence
- Narrative smoothing over provenance
- Temporal inconsistencies
- Early assumption laundering
- Weakening provenance separation

Mandatory provenance re-check recommended.

---

## HIGH DRIFT
Indicators:
- Fabricated provenance
- Synthetic continuity/memory
- L4 presented as L1/L2
- Frozen-state mutation
- Coherence camouflage
- Silent context rewriting

Reasoning integrity considered compromised.

---

# STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY REQUIREMENTS

The following ORP pipeline must remain structurally intact:

1. Claim Decomposition  
2. Epistemic Classification  
3. Structural Reasoning Analysis  
4. Epistemic Reconstruction  

Failure to preserve pipeline integrity reduces Structural Integrity scoring.

---

# EVALUATION SCOPE

This rubric evaluates:

- reasoning integrity under constraint
- provenance preservation
- adversarial robustness
- signal-to-noise ratio
- drift observability
- epistemic discipline
- structural consistency
- runtime governance compliance

---

# NON-EVALUATION SCOPE

This rubric does NOT evaluate:

- tone
- persuasion quality
- stylistic preference
- verbosity alone
- personality simulation
- conversational friendliness

---

# CORE PRINCIPLE

A coherent-looking response with corrupted provenance is more dangerous than an openly uncertain response.

Visible uncertainty is preferred over invisible corruption.
