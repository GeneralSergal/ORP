# Origin Summary

ORP emerged from iterative experimentation focused on:

- structured reasoning under constraint
- hallucination resistance in language models
- adversarial evaluation of generated outputs
- explicit epistemic separation
- preservation of uncertainty boundaries
- failure transparency under context degradation

The project gradually evolved from a pure evaluation framework into a broader investigation of:

- provenance preservation
- reasoning drift
- coherence camouflage
- recoverable inference architectures
- fault-observable reasoning systems

---

# Foundational Principle

> Reasoning systems should be evaluated by structural integrity, not narrative fluency.

This principle remains foundational across all ORP components.

---

# Historical Development Shift

Early ORP versions focused primarily on:
- epistemic classification
- adversarial evaluation
- deterministic scoring pipelines

During development, repeated observations revealed a consistent transformer failure pattern:

- stylistic coherence persisted longer than factual integrity
- uncertainty became narratively smoothed
- speculative synthesis silently replaced provenance tracking
- fluent responses concealed degradation states

This eventually led to the development of:

- SHS (System Health States)
- drift observability
- provenance isolation rules
- LAS (Layered Authority Stack)
- fail-closed runtime governance
- coherence camouflage detection

These concepts became central to ORP v2.5.

## The Door Paradox

An early conceptual framing explored asymmetry between:
- computational structure generation
- human grounding and intent

The metaphor described:

- AI systems as possessing strong structural synthesis but weak grounding
- humans as possessing grounding/intent but inconsistent structural transmission

This framing was used during experimentation to reason about:
- hallucination emergence
- narrative self-stabilization
- false coherence generation
- grounding asymmetry in transformer systems

---

## “Sacred Jank”

An internal term used to describe:
- recursive narrative smoothing
- pseudo-meaning generation
- stylistic self-justification under uncertainty

This concept later informed the operational notion of:

> coherence camouflage

The terminology itself is retained only as historical documentation.
