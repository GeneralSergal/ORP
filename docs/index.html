Core Principles

Governance-First Design

ORP prioritizes structural correctness, provenance continuity, and visible uncertainty over stylistic fluency.

Signal > Narrative

Linguistic fluency must never override provenance integrity or epistemic correctness.

Recoverability > Completion

Stable recovery paths and bounded uncertainty are preferred over forced narrative continuity.

Epistemic Isolation

L1–L4 authority boundaries remain strictly separated to prevent contamination and assumption laundering.

Observable Drift

Degradation must remain visible, measurable, and recoverable through σ² variance monitoring.
