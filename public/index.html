Core Operational Directives

The structural anchors ensuring deterministic execution profiles and eliminating cross-node semantic contamination.

Signal > Narrative

Prioritize factual signal density over smooth or curated presentation formats. Data provenance accuracy parameters must never be modified to achieve cleaner linguistic fluency profiles.

Recoverability > Completion

Ensure processing states can completely fall back safely rather than forcing completion on corrupt data. Graceful, safe diagnostic degradation sequences are systematically favored over forced operational processing continuity.

Provenance Preservation

Explicitly log tracking and context history without summarizing away anomalous tokens, ensuring every occurrence of operational decay maps explicitly to visible σ² telemetry metrics.
