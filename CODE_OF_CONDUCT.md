# CODE OF CONDUCT

ORP — Open Resonance Protocol

---

## 1. Core Principle

ORP is a structured epistemic evaluation system focused on:

- signal integrity
- provenance preservation
- recoverable reasoning
- resistance to reasoning distortion

All participation should prioritize:

- epistemic accuracy over narrative coherence
- structural integrity over stylistic preference
- explicit uncertainty over hidden speculation
- traceable reasoning over fluent fabrication

---

## 2. Expected Standards

Contributors and participants are expected to:

- preserve epistemic boundaries
- distinguish verified information from inference or speculation
- avoid unsupported causal claims
- expose uncertainty clearly
- maintain structural consistency across system layers
- respect fail-closed reasoning behavior and provenance constraints

Constructive criticism, adversarial testing, and architectural disagreement are welcome when grounded in clear reasoning and evidence.

---

## 3. Prohibited Behavior

### 3.1 Epistemic Violations
- presenting unverified claims as factual
- collapsing epistemic categories
- laundering speculation into verified form
- masking uncertainty through narrative coherence

### 3.2 Structural Violations
- bypassing schema or runtime constraints
- merging responsibilities across architecture layers
- introducing undocumented behavioral changes without alignment

### 3.3 Community Violations
- harassment or personal attacks
- disruptive bad-faith participation
- intentionally misleading benchmark submissions
- fabricated evaluation results or provenance

---

## 4. Enforcement Model

ORP emphasizes structural accountability rather than subjective moderation language.

Violations may result in:
- rejection of contributions
- invalidation of evaluation outputs
- rollback of incompatible architectural changes
- removal of intentionally misleading material

Evaluation focuses on structural correctness, provenance integrity, and system consistency.

---

## 5. Scope

This code of conduct applies to:
- repository discussions
- pull requests
- benchmark contributions
- evaluation artifacts
- architecture proposals
- associated documentation

It complements, but does not override:
- ORP_RUNTIME.md
- EVALUATION_SCHEMA.md
- SYSTEM_MAP.manifest.json
- repository licensing terms

---

## 6. Design Principle

> Visible uncertainty is preferable to invisible corruption.

Signal integrity remains the primary system value.
