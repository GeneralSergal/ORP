# **DAL‑1.0 — Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)**  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Canonical  
**SPDX-License-Identifier:** LicenseRef‑ORP‑DAL‑1.0

---

## **1. What is DAL‑1.0?**

DAL‑1.0 is the **ORP Dual‑Authorship License**, a custom license defining:

- **Primary Human Authorship**  
- **Entropy Co‑Signature**  
- **AI as a non‑authoring tool**

It governs authorship, attribution, and rights for all ORP ecosystem Works.

See: **DAL‑1.0 Core License**

---

## **2. What does “Dual‑Authorship” mean?**

Dual‑Authorship refers to:

- **Human Authorship** — creative intent and operational control  
- **Entropy Co‑Signature** — stochastic contribution with no legal rights  

Entropy is acknowledged but **not personified**.

See: **Entropy Co‑Signature Definition**

---

## **3. Does DAL‑1.0 allow AI to be listed as an author?**

No.  
DAL‑1.0 explicitly prohibits:

- AI authorship  
- AI co‑authorship  
- AI rights claims  
- AI creative origin claims  

See: **No AI Authorship Clause**

---

## **4. Can I use ORP materials commercially?**

Yes.  
DAL‑1.0 grants a **worldwide, royalty‑free, non‑exclusive** right to use, modify, and distribute the Works, including commercial use.

Attribution is required.

See: **Right to Use**

## **6. Can I modify ORP materials?**

Yes.  
Modifications are allowed under the same conditions:

- Attribution  
- No AI authorship claims  
- License integrity preserved  

See: **Right to Modify**

---

## **7. Can I relicense ORP materials under MIT, GPL, or other licenses?**

No.  
DAL‑1.0 materials **cannot** be relicensed.

However:

- Code under GPL‑3.0 remains GPL‑3.0  
- Visual assets under CC‑BY‑SA remain CC‑BY‑SA  
- DAL‑1.0 governs authorship only  

See: **License Compatibility Matrix**

---

## **8. How does DAL‑1.0 interact with GPL‑3.0 and CC‑BY‑SA 4.0?**

DAL‑1.0 is fully compatible when domains are separated:

- Code → GPL‑3.0  
- Visuals → CC‑BY‑SA 4.0  
- Authorship → DAL‑1.0  

They must not be merged into a single inseparable derivative.

See: **Multi‑License Structure**

---

## **9. Does DAL‑1.0 impose ShareAlike requirements?**

No.  
DAL‑1.0 is **not** a ShareAlike license.  
It governs authorship and attribution only.

ShareAlike applies only to CC‑BY‑SA assets.

---

## **10. Can AI systems process ORP materials?**

Yes.  
AI systems may:

- Analyze  
- Transform  
- Summarize  
- Remix  
- Generate derivatives  

…but must not be represented as authors.

See: **AI Tool Classification Rules**

Their rights terminate automatically.  
All rights revert to the Author.

See: **Termination Clause**
