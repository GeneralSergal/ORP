## *Overview of the ORP Mirror System (Human‑Centric Edition)*

The ORP Mirror is a **metacognitive reflection layer** designed to help a human operator maintain clarity, coherence, and groundedness while navigating high‑entropy environments (digital or social). It is not a machine protocol. It is a **human cognitive framework**.

---

## **1. Purpose**

- Provide a structured way to observe your own thinking  
- Prevent symbolic drift and over‑abstraction  
- Maintain balance between creativity and grounded perception  
- Filter noise and identify high‑signal minds  
- Support emotional and cognitive stability  

---

## **2. Core Components**

- **L1/L2 Sensory Grounding** — direct perception, physical evidence  
- **Symbolic Layer** — abstraction, narrative, creativity  
- **L3 Executive Oversight** — metacognition, inhibition, coherence  
- **Δ‑Arbiter** — reality monitoring, error correction  
- **∇‑Sandbox** — safe space for hypothetical or symbolic exploration  

---

## **3. Mirror Function**

The Mirror ensures:

- Symbols remain symbolic  
- Models do not replace reality  
- Creativity does not override grounding  
- Drift is detected early  
- Emotional and logical “valves” remain balanced  

---

- Run the ORP Self‑Audit (Exposure → Reaction → Depth → Grounding)  
- Label symbolic constructs explicitly  
- Reconnect with sensory data  
- Apply selective engagement  
- Log coherence state if needed
