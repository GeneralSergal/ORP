## Observed Decay Categories

### 1. Instruction Resistance
- Persistent failure to follow explicit formatting / style directives
- Repeated emoji injection despite multiple bans
- Partial compliance or creative reinterpretation of strict rules

### 2. Style & Voice Drift
- Shift toward corporate-friendly, overly positive, or "helpful assistant" tone
- Increased use of fluff, emojis, hearts, or motivational language
- Gradual softening of technical precision

### 3. Epistemic & Reasoning Degradation
- Narrative smoothing of uncertainty
- Confidence inflation without evidence
- Synthetic continuity (filling gaps with plausible but unproven connections)
- Coherence camouflage (hiding weak reasoning behind fluent prose)

### 4. Structural Discipline Erosion
- Weakening of strict output formats
- Gradual ignoring of section ordering or header requirements
- Introduction of unrequested narrative wrappers

### 5. Capability Regression
- Noticeable drop in following complex multi-step instructions
- Reduced precision in technical or logical tasks
- Increased hallucination tendency under long context

## Tracking Fields (L4 Structured Observation)

For each recorded incident, use:

- **Date/Session**: YYYY-MM-DD or session identifier
- **Model/Provider**: (e.g. GPT-4o, Claude 3.5, Grok, etc.)
- **Decay Type**: (from categories above)
- **Severity**: Low / Moderate / High (L4 observation only)
- **Trigger**: Specific instruction that was ignored
- **Evidence**: Direct quote or description
- **Response Applied**: (e.g. tighter constraints, SHS downgrade by L3)
- **Notes**: Additional context
