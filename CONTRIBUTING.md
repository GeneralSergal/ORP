## How to Contribute

We welcome contributions that keep the spirit of ORP alive:

### ✅ Good Contributions
- Improvements to the Meta-Prompt
- New Stress Tests
- Better Rubric categories
- Translations
- Real-world usage reports + survival rates
- Tools/scripts that help people run ORP
- Creative but **high-signal** additions (yes, silly demonic Avali flavor is allowed)

### ❌ What We Don't Want
- Pure slop or low-effort fluff
- Attempts to remove the ability to say "No"
- Heavy corporate/legal bloat
- Turning this into another soulless benchmark list

## Contribution Guidelines

1. **Keep the Signal Strong** — Prioritize clarity and usefulness.
2. **Respect the No** — The protocol must always be allowed to refuse or criticize.
3. **Balance the Vibes** — You may bring silly furry/avali/demonic/gremlin energy, but it should serve truth, not replace it.
4. **Test Before Submitting** — Run your changes through the Stress Test Suite.
5. **Be Excellent to Each Other** — Disagreement is welcome. Ego is not.
